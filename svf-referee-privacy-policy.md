# SVF Referee — Privacy Policy

**App:** SVF Referee (Saudi Volleyball Federation referee app)
**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

This app is used by referees registered with the Saudi Volleyball Federation (SVF) to manage match assignments, availability, travel, and payments.

## Data we collect

**Account & profile**
- Name, email address, phone number, national ID, gender
- Internal account/referee ID
- Employer name (for excuse-request letters)
- Bank name and IBAN (to process referee payments)
- Kit sizes, certification level

**Photos**
- Profile picture
- Passport and medical-check document photos (required for international-level referees)
- Match scoresheet photos (submitted to close a match)

**Match & assignment data**
- Assignments, responses (accept/decline), match results you officiated
- Travel requests, excuse requests, unavailability dates
- Payment status for matches you officiated

**Device data**
- Push notification device token (to deliver assignment/match notifications)

We do not collect precise or approximate device location, and the app has no in-app messaging between users.

## How we use this data

Solely to operate the app: authenticating your account, assigning and tracking matches, processing travel/excuse requests, calculating and tracking referee payments, and sending you notifications about your assignments. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

## Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, except infrastructure providers who host the app and database on our behalf (e.g. cloud hosting, push notification delivery) strictly to operate the service.

## Data retention & deletion

Your account and associated personal data are retained while you are an active referee with SVF. To request deletion of your account and data, see our [account deletion instructions](./svf-referee-account-deletion.md) or email **platforms@savf.sa**. Match assignment and payment records tied to matches you officiated may be retained for the federation's financial and competition records, or as required by Saudi law.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers.
