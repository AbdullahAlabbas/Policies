# Privacy Policy — Monee
**Effective date:** October 29, 2025

**Monee** (“the App”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is a personal budgeting app that runs entirely on your device.  
This policy explains what information we (do not) collect, how on-device data is used, and your choices.

---

## Summary (plain language)

- We **do not collect, store, or share** your personal data on our servers.  
- Your budgets, purchases, settings, and preferences are **saved only on your device** using local storage.  
- We don’t use advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.  
- Notifications are **optional** and generated locally on your device.  
- You can delete all in-app data at any time inside the app.

---

## Information We Process

### On-device data (not sent to us)
The App saves the following **only on your device**:

- Budget settings (monthly budget amount, start day)
- Purchases you add (dates, categories, amounts)
- Display preferences (currency, dark mode, language)
- Profile visuals (avatar, name you enter)
- App icon choice
- Notification preferences (daily reminder on/off)

Stored using Flutter `SharedPreferences` (local storage).  
Retained until you clear data or uninstall the app.

### Notifications (optional)
If enabled, local notifications remind you about budgeting events.  
They are created and stored on your device, never sent externally.

### Widgets & Deep Links
Budget summary widgets and deep links operate entirely locally; no external data transfer occurs.

---

## Information We Do **Not** Collect

- No accounts or sign-ins  
- No emails, phone numbers, or contact info  
- No analytics, ads, or trackers  
- No location, contacts, or photos  
- No data shared or sold

## Third-Party Services

The app may use open-source libraries for UI, localization, and icons — all of which operate locally and never send your data externally.

> **Note:** If you use iCloud or Android Auto Backup, your system might include app data in its encrypted backups. That’s controlled by your device, not us.

## Your Choices

- **Clear Data:** Settings → *Clear Data* to erase everything.  
- **Notifications:** Turn off in Settings or your phone’s system app settings.  
- **Language/Currency:** Change anytime in Settings.
