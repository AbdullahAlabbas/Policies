# SVF Referee — Account Deletion

**App:** SVF Referee (Saudi Volleyball Federation referee app)

## How to request deletion

Email **platforms@savf.sa** from the address associated with your referee account and ask for your account to be deleted. Include your full name and the phone number or email you use to sign in. Requests are processed manually and completed within 30 days.

The in-app **Profile → Delete account** button signs you out and temporarily locks sign-in, but does not itself erase your data — a request to the email above is required to complete deletion.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile (name, contact details, national ID, bank IBAN, kit sizes, uploaded documents)
- Your account credentials and sign-in history
- Notifications and device push tokens

## What may be retained

- Match assignment and payment records tied to matches you officiated, kept for the federation's financial and competition records
- Data the federation is required to retain under Saudi law or an active dispute/audit
